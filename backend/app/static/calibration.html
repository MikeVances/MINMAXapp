Calibrate building heat loss and calculate additional combustion-air demand for open-flame gas heaters. Calibration is performed once; results are used to automatically adjust minimum ventilation when heaters are active.

Enter the building dimensions. Volume is calculated automatically.

Step 2: Heating equipment

Specify heater type and total capacity. For open-flame gas heaters the system automatically adds combustion air (≈95 m³/h per 100 kW).

ℹ️ Gas heater combustion air:
  • Natural gas consumption: ~10 m³/h per 100 kW
  • Combustion air required: ~9.5 m³ per m³ of gas
  • Total: ~95 m³/h of air per 100 kW

Step 3: Cooling test (calibration)

Procedure (empty house only):
1. Heat the building to a known temperature (e.g. 30 °C)
2. Turn off all heating and ventilation
3. Record temperature after 5–10 minutes
4. Enter the data below to calculate the UA heat-loss coefficient
